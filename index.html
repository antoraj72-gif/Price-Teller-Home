How it works

Discounts are stacked – never added

Each scheme is taken off the price left after the one before it. That is how your company calculates, so that is how Price Teller calculates.

  • Your own discount names and order – add a new scheme any time
  • Only the final price is rounded, to the nearest rupee
  • Every step shown, so anyone can check the maths

FAQ

Questions people ask

What does Price Teller do?

It works out the exact net landing cost (Final NLC) of any model for any channel and dealer, applying every scheme discount in the right order. It also prices whole lists of models, totals quantity and value, makes quotations and exports reports.

Which price list files can I upload?

Excel (.xlsx / .xls, including many sheets), CSV, PDF, text, and photos or screenshots of a price list (JPG / PNG). You can upload several files at once, or add a model by hand.

How are the discounts calculated?

One after another: each discount is taken off the price left after the previous one, and only the final price is rounded to the nearest rupee. The full breakdown is shown for every calculation.

Can I add a new scheme or change the order?

Yes. In Settings you can add, rename or remove discount headers, set a default % and choose the order they are applied in.

Do I have to type each dealer's scheme every time?

No. Save each dealer's scheme once – upload an Excel file or add it by hand. The Dealer and Bulk calculators then apply it automatically; only manual discounts such as CD are typed at the time.

Can I calculate many models together?

Yes – the Bulk calculator takes a pasted or uploaded list of models (or a whole category) and prices each one separately. Send the results straight to Quantity & Value for the order total.

Can it make quotations with my company logo?

Yes. Upload your company's sample quotation once and Price Teller fills it in for every new customer, with your logo and letterhead. You check the Word version, approve it, and get the final PDF to share or print.

Does it need internet? Is my price list safe?

No internet is needed after installing. Your price lists, dealers and quotations stay only on your device – nothing is sent to any server.

How do I get access?

Install the app and ask the owner for your unlock key. The key is made for your device; after unlocking you open the app with your fingerprint or your own password.

How do I install it on iPhone or a laptop?

Android: tap “Install on Android”. iPhone: open the app link in Safari, tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen”. Windows or Mac: open the link in Chrome or Edge and click the install icon in the address bar.

How do I get new updates?

Open Settings → Software Update. Your saved price lists, dealers and quotations stay as they are.
